# What the agent established

- (active) A k=16 pointwise Factorization Machine trained with Adam at lr=0.001 on user_id, video_id, author_id, tab, and duration_bucket reproduces the official baseline within seed noise (primary 0.6020 versus 0.6016), so it remains a valid reference but provides no measurable improvement over the baseline. [iters 1]
- (active) The tested 18-field DeepFM configuration, selected at epoch 4 and blended with the harness at alpha 0.75, provides no measurable ranking gain over the five-field FM reference (primary 0.6032 versus 0.6020, a difference of 0.0012 inside seed noise), despite substantially greater GPU cost. [iters 1,2]
- (active) The tested LightGBM-enhanced candidate using 71 safe categorical and transformed numeric features, including 24 train-only video/author history features, and an internal rank blend selected at alpha 0.10 provides no measurable gain over the incumbent DeepFM candidate (primary 0.6034 versus 0.6032, a difference of 0.0002 inside seed noise) [iters 2,3]
- (active) Adding a sparse wide model with explicit user×video, user×author, user×tag, and user×context crosses through a validation-selected z-score blend provides no measurable gain over the LightGBM incumbent (primary 0.6040 versus 0.6034, a difference of about 0.0006 inside seed noise). [iters 3,4]
- (active) The tested sparse wide model is not competitive as a standalone ranker: its best observed primary was 0.5967, more than 0.006 below the 0.6034 incumbent, and its ranking quality declined to 0.5710 while weighted logloss continued improving, indicating that further pointwise-loss training of this formulation worsens within-user ranking. [iters 4]
- (active) Adding vectorized chronological, daily, session, and same-timestamp batch-position features through a validation-selected LightGBM z-blend provides no measurable gain over the 0.6040 incumbent: the selected alpha-0.30 blend reached primary 0.6048, an increase of about 0.0007 inside seed noise. [iters 4,5]
- (active) The tested position-feature LightGBM is not competitive as a standalone ranker, with validation primary 0.5655 versus approximately 0.6040 for the incumbent; any useful signal from these impression-context features is limited to a weak blend under the tested formulation. [iters 5]
- (active) Absolute chronological-count features exhibit substantial train-to-validation distribution shift: mean daily total falls from 18.042 to 3.257 and mean session total from 5.056 to 1.506, while mean same-timestamp batch total changes only from 1.165 to 1.060. [iters 5]
- (active) The tested low-rank DCN-V2 tower over 18 categorical fields, 37 numeric features, and 24 leakage-free history features provides no measurable gain over the 0.6048 incumbent: the epoch-3 rank blend selected at alpha 0.50 reached internal primary 0.6056 and final scored primary 0.6057, only about 0.0009 higher and inside seed noise. [iters 5,7]
- (active) Under the tested DCN-V2 formulation, explicit bounded-degree interactions are not proven superior as a standalone ranker: the best raw epoch primary was 0.6041 versus 0.6048 for the incumbent, while rank aggregation raised it only to 0.6056, with both differences inside seed noise. [iters 7]
- (active) The tested hierarchical Bayesian user×duration/context response curves provide no measurable incremental gain over the 0.605669 incumbent: the selected duration_quantile_s12_blend_0.075 candidate reached internal primary 0.605673 and final scored primary 0.6057, effectively unchanged and far inside seed noise. [iters 7,8]
- (active) Pairwise tab/tag response curves are not competitive as standalone rankers under the tested hierarchical-statistics formulation: the best pair curve, tab_tag_s35, achieved primary 0.5638 versus approximately 0.6057 for the incumbent. [iters 8]
- (active) The current aggregated candidate is measurably above both the official 0.6016 baseline and the 0.6020 five-field FM reference, scoring primary 0.6057; however, neither the DCN-V2 addition nor the subsequent hierarchical user×duration/context statistics produced a measurable incremental gain over its preceding incumbent. [iters 1,5,7,8]
